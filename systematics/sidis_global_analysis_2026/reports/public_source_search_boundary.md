# Public SIDIS source-search boundary

Search date: 2026-08-26. HEPData HERMES and COMPASS multiplicity collections
and a broad SIDIS/multiplicity query were checked. The included registry holds
the seven unpolarized multiplicity records with a transverse-momentum axis or
a clearly useful collinear complement. Omitted records are tracked in
`config/excluded_public_sources.json`.

The HERMES nuclear-hadronization collection (record 13387) is not merged with
free-proton/deuteron data because it requires a nuclear-hadronization model.
Spin-dependent SIDIS asymmetry records, including COMPASS Sivers and JLab
Hall-A polarized measurements, are outside the present unpolarized scope and
remain future extensions.
